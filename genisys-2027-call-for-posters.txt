Research posters & presentations
GeniSys 2027
Exploring Synergy among Next-Generation AI, Systems, and Networking for a Sustainable Future

Rice University | Houston, Texas
Venue: Ralph S. O’Connor Building for Engineering and Science
Room: Fifth-floor conference room
Schedule: https://genisys27.github.io/schedule.html
Workshop hours: 9:00 am–5:00 pm in Houston (Central Time); tentative
Workshop date: Mid-February 2027
Submission deadline: January 10, 2027
Poster submission link: https://rice.app.box.com/f/0370111606fc4a1da368d135b485fb80
Research presentation submission link: https://rice.app.box.com/f/4326bc92911c49e6beec9122233712e3

GeniSys 2027 invites research posters and research presentations exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, computing systems, and networking. Share your research, receive feedback, practice presenting to a broader audience, and meet potential collaborators across AI, systems, and networking.

TOPICS OF INTEREST
Topics include, but are not limited to:
- AI algorithm design and agent systems
- Scalable AI training and efficient distributed inference
- GPU and heterogeneous system optimization
- Communication-efficient learning and networking for AI
- Reliable and resilient AI infrastructure
- Energy-efficient and sustainable computing
- Secure AI deployment and cross-layer co-design

ELIGIBLE WORK
Both research posters and presentations are open to undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students and postdoctoral researchers. Undergraduate and master’s students are especially encouraged to participate. Research in progress, research-oriented course projects, and recent or previously published work are welcome.

DRAFT MATERIALS WELCOME
Submitted slides and posters need not be final; you may refine them before the workshop. All presenters are encouraged to explain the research problem, approach, and key results or insights and highlight questions for discussion with the workshop community.

WORKSHOP EXPERIENCE
The tentative one-day program runs from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm in Houston (Central Time), with all sessions held sequentially. It includes three invited speaker slots, up to nine research presentations, a 75-minute lunch and discussion, two 30-minute coffee breaks, and a dedicated poster and demo session from 4:00 to 5:00 pm. The final talk allocation across career stages and research groups will reflect submissions.

Invited speaker slots run from 9:00–9:45 am, 11:00–11:45 am, and 1:45–2:30 pm. A brief welcome is included in the first slot. Introductions, Q&A, and transitions are included in each 45-minute slot. Speakers and topics will be announced.

Research presentations occupy three sessions: 9:45–10:30 am, 1:00–1:45 pm, and 3:00–3:45 pm. Each session has at most three talks. Each presenter has a 15-minute slot including approximately 10 minutes presenting, followed by Q&A and transition. Presentation decks may contain a maximum of 15 slides. Open discussion and transition to posters run from 3:45 to 4:00 pm.

Coffee and conversation breaks run from 10:30 to 11:00 am and 2:30 to 3:00 pm. Lunch and discussion run from 11:45 am to 1:00 pm. The workshop finishes with posters and demos from 4:00 to 5:00 pm.
