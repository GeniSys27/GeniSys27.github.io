GeniSys 2027 invites research posters exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, computing systems, and networking. We welcome recent research and previously published work. The poster session offers an opportunity to share results, exchange ideas, and connect with researchers, practitioners, and students across these communities.

TOPICS OF INTEREST
Topics include, but are not limited to:
- Scalable AI training and efficient distributed inference
- GPU and heterogeneous system optimization
- Communication-efficient learning and networking for AI
- Reliable and resilient AI infrastructure
- Energy-efficient and sustainable computing
- Secure AI deployment and cross-layer co-design

ELIGIBLE WORK
Both recent research and previously published work are welcome. Presenters are encouraged to explain the research problem, approach, and key results or insights, and to highlight questions for discussion with the workshop community.

WORKSHOP EXPERIENCE
The tentative one-day program runs from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Houston (Central Time), with all sessions held sequentially. It includes three external speakers, eight undergraduate/master’s presentations, six PhD presentations from different research groups, lunch from 12:30 to 1:15 pm, and a dedicated poster/demo session from 4:00 to 5:00 pm.

External speakers have 45-minute slots at 9:00–9:45 am, 11:45 am–12:30 pm, and 1:15–2:00 pm. Introductions, Q&A, and transitions are included in each slot. Speakers and topics are to be announced.

Undergraduate/master’s presentations occupy two one-hour sessions: 10:45–11:45 am and 3:00–4:00 pm, with four students in each session. Each student has a 15-minute slot. Presentation decks must contain fewer than 15 slides (maximum 14).

PhD presentations occupy two 45-minute sessions: 9:45–10:30 am and 2:00–2:45 pm, with three speakers in each. The proposed format is 15 minutes per speaker. For all student talks, the suggested format is 10–12 minutes presenting, with the remaining time for Q&A and transition.

Coffee breaks run from 10:30 to 10:45 am and 2:45 to 3:00 pm. The workshop finishes with posters and demos from 4:00 to 5:00 pm. Hybrid participation is planned; arrangements for remote poster presentations will be announced.
